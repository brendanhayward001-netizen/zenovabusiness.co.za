The platform

Two products. One system of record.

Margaret runs the two jobs that quietly consume a manager's week: deciding who works when, and keeping the paperwork straight. Use either on its own, or both together.

Margaret Scheduler

Rosters built from your trading pattern

We start with your own point-of-sale history and work out when customers actually arrive — not when everyone assumes they do. The roster is then built to meet that demand within the hours you already pay for.

  • Shift patterns and rest-day cycles that rotate fairly
  • Working-time limits and meal breaks applied as you build
  • Leave, cover and public holidays handled in one place
  • Printable rosters for the noticeboard, live view for managers
Margaret HR & IR

Personnel records you can produce on demand

Every employee file in one place, with the system telling you what is missing rather than you finding out at the worst moment. Contracts, certificates and disciplinary records filed against the right person, with an audit trail.

  • Documents filed automatically as they arrive
  • A live view of which files are complete and which are not
  • Expiry tracking for tickets, permits and certificates
  • Disciplinary and grievance matters recorded from start to finish
How it works

We do the setup. You run the business.

Onboarding is done with you, not handed to you as a login and a manual. Most clients are live within a few weeks.

01

We look at what you have

Trading data, current rosters, existing personnel files. We tell you what we find, including the parts you would rather we didn't.

02

We build your setup

Departments, shift patterns, roles and document requirements configured to how your business actually operates.

03

We load your people

Staff and existing documents brought in and filed. You see the gaps immediately, with a plan for closing them.

04

You take it from there

Managers work in the browser or over WhatsApp. We stay reachable, and we keep improving the platform behind you.

Pricing

Priced per person, not per seat.

You pay for the people on your payroll, not for how many managers need to log in. Larger workforces move to lower per-person rates automatically.

Every quote is written down before anything starts, and there is no charge while we are still setting you up. Ask us for a figure and you will get one — we would rather have that conversation than publish a number that does not fit your business.

Ask for a quote
  • A once-off setup fee Covers the analysis, configuration and loading of your existing records. Charged per department, so a single site costs less than a group.
  • A monthly rate per employee Banded, so the rate drops as headcount rises. A minimum monthly figure applies to each band.
  • No charge for extra users Add as many managers, supervisors and administrators as the business needs.
  • No lock-in on the software Month to month. Your data stays yours, and you can have it back on request.
About Zenova

Built here, for how business is actually run here.

Zenova Business Solutions is a South African software company based in Gqeberha. We build and own the Margaret platform, and we work directly with the people who use it — store managers, operations managers and payroll administrators.

South African by design

Working hours, breaks, Sunday and public holiday rules, and record-keeping obligations are built into the platform rather than bolted on. Personal information is handled in line with POPIA, and clients are told exactly what we hold and why.

Employment expertise alongside the software

Margaret is delivered in partnership with an established HR and industrial relations practice. Where a matter needs a practitioner — chairing an enquiry, or representing an employer at the CCMA — that expertise sits alongside the platform rather than being something you have to go and find.

A supermarket group using Margaret Scheduler cut its worst coverage gaps by more than half — without adding a single paid hour to the roster.
